About Student Help Desk

The Student Help Desk Portal is a centralized platform designed to help students communicate with college departments.


Students can submit requests, report campus problems and track their ticket status.


Our Objectives


• Improve communication between students and staff.


• Provide a centralized complaint system.


• Reduce manual complaint management.


• Improve transparency and accountability.
